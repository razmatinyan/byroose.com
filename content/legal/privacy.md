## Who we are

forrrow is a creative agency based in Yerevan, Armenia. We create AI content and build websites for brands. In this policy, "forrrow", "we", "us", and "our" mean the forrrow agency, and "you" means anyone who visits forrrow.com, contacts us, or works with us.

We are responsible for the personal data described in this policy. If you have a question about it, write to us at [info@forrrow.com](mailto:info@forrrow.com).

## Scope of this policy

This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. It covers:

- Visits to forrrow.com and its pages
- Messages you send us through the contact form or by email
- Information we handle while we deliver AI content creation or web development work for a client

It does not cover websites, platforms, or services run by other companies, even when we link to them. Their own privacy policies apply there.

## Information we collect

We collect as little personal data as we need to run the site, answer you, and deliver our work.

### Information you give us

- **Contact form messages.** When you use the form on our contact page, we receive your name, your email address, and the idea you describe.
- **Emails and calls.** When you write to us or speak with us, we receive your name, contact details, company, and whatever you choose to share about your project, timeline, or budget.
- **Project information.** When you become a client, we receive the details needed to plan, deliver, and invoice the work, such as contact people, billing details, and the materials described in [Client materials and AI tools](#client-materials-and-ai-tools).

### Information collected automatically

- **Technical data.** Like any website, our hosting and delivery providers process technical data when your browser requests a page. This includes your IP address, browser and device type, the page you requested, the referring page, and the time of the request. It is used to deliver the site, keep it secure, and diagnose errors.
- **Abuse protection.** When you send the contact form, we hold your IP address in temporary server memory for up to 10 minutes to limit repeated submissions and block spam.
- **Your cookie choice.** We store your answer to our cookie notice in a cookie, as described in [Cookies and similar technologies](#cookies-and-similar-technologies).

We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking tools on forrrow.com. Our fonts and icons are served from our own domain, so loading a page does not send your data to font or icon services.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal data, such as health information, political opinions, or financial account numbers. Please do not send it to us through the contact form or by email.

## How we use your information

We use personal data only for clear, specific purposes:

- To reply to your message and discuss a possible project
- To prepare proposals, agree on scope, and deliver the work you hire us for
- To send invoices, receive payments, and keep business records
- To run, protect, and improve forrrow.com, including preventing spam and abuse
- To remember your cookie choice
- To meet our legal, tax, and accounting obligations, and to establish or defend legal claims

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use it for automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you, and we do not add you to a mailing list. If we ever introduce a newsletter, you will only receive it if you sign up for it.

## Legal bases for processing

Where data protection laws such as the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation apply, we rely on these legal bases:

- **Contract.** We process data to take steps you request before an agreement and to perform our agreement with you.
- **Legitimate interests.** We process data to answer enquiries, run and secure our website, prevent abuse, and manage our business, where those interests are not overridden by your rights.
- **Consent.** We rely on your consent where the law requires it, for example for any optional cookies we might add in the future. You can withdraw consent at any time.
- **Legal obligation.** We process data when we must keep records or respond to lawful requests from authorities.

## Client materials and AI tools

To create content and build websites, our clients share materials with us, such as logos, brand guidelines, product photos, footage, copy, and access to existing accounts. These materials can contain personal data, for example images of people or customer information.

- **Use limited to the work.** We use client materials only to deliver the work we agreed on. We do not sell them, share them with other clients, or use them for unrelated purposes.
- **AI tools.** Our AI content creation work uses third-party tools to generate and edit images, video, and other content. We upload only the materials a project needs to the AI tools we use.
- **People in your materials.** If your materials show identifiable people, you confirm that you have their permission for us to use those materials.
- **No imitation of real people.** We do not use AI to imitate real, identifiable people. AI characters we create, such as UGC-style creators, are original and do not depict real people.
- **Your website's visitors.** When we build or maintain a website for you, you decide how your visitors' data is collected and used. For that data, we act on your instructions and under your agreement with us.
- **Account access.** When you give us access to hosting, content management, or other accounts, we use it only for the work, and we recommend that you remove or rotate our access once the handover is complete.
- **Showing our work.** We publish client names, projects, and results on our website only with the client's approval.

## Cookies and similar technologies

A cookie is a small text file that a website stores in your browser. forrrow.com uses one cookie:

- **forrrow-cookie-consent.** Stores whether you accepted or declined our cookie notice, so the notice does not appear on every visit. It is set only when you answer the notice and lasts for one year.

This cookie does not identify you and is not used for tracking. We do not set analytics or advertising cookies. If we add any tool that uses optional cookies in the future, it will run only after you choose "Accept all", and we will update this policy first.

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings at any time. If you delete our cookie, the notice will appear again on your next visit.

## How we share information

We share personal data only when it is needed for the purposes in this policy:

- **Service providers.** We use trusted providers for website hosting and delivery, email delivery, email and file storage, AI content generation, and project and business tools. They process data on our behalf and only for the services they provide to us.
- **Professional advisers.** We may share information with accountants, lawyers, or insurers when it is necessary for our business and under a duty of confidentiality.
- **Legal requirements.** We may disclose information when the law requires it, or when it is necessary to protect our rights, our clients, or the public.
- **Business changes.** If forrrow is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, personal data may transfer as part of that transaction, and this policy will continue to protect it.

We never sell personal data or share it with third parties for their own marketing.

## International transfers

We are based in Armenia, and some of our service providers store or process data in other countries, including the United States and member states of the European Union. These countries may have data protection laws that differ from the laws where you live.

When we transfer personal data internationally, we use providers that apply recognized safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent contractual protections, and we limit the data transferred to what the service needs.

## How long we keep information

We keep personal data only as long as we need it for the purpose we collected it for:

- **Enquiries.** Messages that do not lead to a project are kept for as long as reasonably needed to follow up, then deleted.
- **Client records.** Contracts, project correspondence, and invoices are kept for the length of the engagement and afterwards for as long as accounting, tax, and other legal obligations require.
- **Client materials.** Project files are kept while we deliver and support the work. You can ask us to delete or return them when the engagement ends, unless we must keep a copy by law.
- **Abuse protection data.** IP addresses used to limit contact form submissions are cleared from memory within 10 minutes.
- **Your cookie choice.** The consent cookie expires after one year.
- **Technical logs.** Our hosting providers keep request logs for a limited period under their own retention schedules, for security and troubleshooting.

## How we protect information

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal data. The site is served only over encrypted HTTPS connections, contact form input is validated on our server, and repeated submissions are limited. Access to client materials and our business accounts is restricted to the people who need it for the work, and we protect those accounts with strong authentication.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a data breach affects your personal data and the law requires us to tell you, we will notify you and the relevant authority without undue delay.

## Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- Ask whether we hold personal data about you and receive a copy of it
- Ask us to correct data that is inaccurate or incomplete
- Ask us to delete your data
- Ask us to restrict how we use your data
- Object to processing based on our legitimate interests
- Receive the data you gave us in a structured, commonly used format, or have it sent to another organization
- Withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on consent

To use any of these rights, email [info@forrrow.com](mailto:info@forrrow.com). We may ask you to confirm your identity before we act on the request, and we will reply within one month. We do not charge a fee unless a request is clearly unfounded or excessive.

If you believe we have not handled your data properly, please contact us first so we can try to resolve it. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. In Armenia, this is the Personal Data Protection Agency of the Ministry of Justice. In the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you can contact the supervisory authority where you live or work.

## Children's privacy

forrrow.com and our services are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

## Links to other websites

Our site links to other websites, such as our social media profiles and the live websites of projects we have delivered. When you follow those links, the other site's privacy policy applies. We are not responsible for how those websites handle personal data.
